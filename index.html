Site for Sale Subject to Planning Permission

Lackandarra, Colligan, Dungarvan, Co. Waterford

Folio No. WD29852F | Approx. 0.375 Hectares / 0.93 Acres

Denise Radley Auctioneers are delighted to offer for sale this attractive 0.375 hectare (approximately 0.93 acre) site, being sold subject to planning permission.

The site forms part of Folio WD29852F and is situated at Lackendarra, Colligan, in a particularly picturesque and peaceful part of Co. Waterford.

Lackendarra enjoys an excellent rural location just off the main Clonmel–Dungarvan Road, approximately 15 minutes from Dungarvan and 25 minutes from Clonmel, making it an ideal location for those seeking countryside living while remaining within convenient reach of both towns.

One of the great attractions of the location is its proximity to the beautiful Colligan Woods, offering wonderful woodland walks and outdoor recreation practically on the doorstep. The surrounding countryside is exceptionally scenic and provides an attractive setting for a future family home, subject to obtaining the necessary planning permission.

The area is also known for its strong community spirit and is well served by local amenities. There is a good primary school nearby, while a choice of secondary schools is available in both Dungarvan and Clonmel.

This is an excellent opportunity for anyone hoping to build their own home in a scenic countryside location with convenient access to Dungarvan and Clonmel.
